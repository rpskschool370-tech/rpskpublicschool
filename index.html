About Us

25+ Years of Quality Education

RPSK Public School, Karman Tola, Ara, Bihar is committed to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined, and student-friendly environment. The school follows the CBSE pattern and focuses on academic excellence, moral values, creativity, and the overall development of every child.

  • Experienced and trained teaching staff
  • Sports, music and art facilities
  • Safe transport and CCTV monitoring

Our Mission (Mission)

To provide every child a strong academic foundation along with moral values.

Our Vision (Vision)

To develop self-reliant, responsible and capable citizens.

Principal's Message
"Education isn't limited to textbooks alone — it teaches the art of living. Our endeavour is that every student moves forward with confidence and strong values."

Principal — R.P.S.K. Public School
